## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

```
Islam Connect gives you the prayer times your masjid actually announces — not
times calculated from your coordinates.

Follow your local masjid and you see exactly what is on their board: azan times,
jamat times, and any announcement they post. When a prayer time arrives, the azan
plays.


PRAYER TIMES FROM YOUR MASJID

• Follow any registered masjid and see its real azan and jamat times
• Announcements from your masjid appear on your home screen
• Find masjids within 7 km, or search by name
• A full weekly schedule, and the next prayer always on screen


AN AZAN ALARM THAT WORKS

Most prayer apps set an alarm and hope. This one keeps a service running that
checks the time itself, because on many Android phones that is the only way an
alarm reliably fires.

• The azan plays at prayer time, even with the screen locked
• Your masjid's own azan recording, if they have uploaded one
• A setup guide that shows exactly which phone settings block alarms — including
  the manufacturer-specific ones most apps never mention
• Juma is a Friday prayer, and is treated as one


THE QUR'AN

• All 114 surahs in the Uthmanic Mushaf script
• Translations in English, Urdu and Hindi
• Recitation audio with adjustable speed
• Read by surah or by juz, page by page like a printed Mushaf
• Sajdah verses marked, with the difference between schools noted
• Your reading position is saved


HADITH

• Six collections: Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah
• Over thirty thousand hadith, in English and Urdu
• Search by hadith number and land on that hadith directly
• Authenticity gradings shown where the collection records them
• Listen to the translation, at your own speed
• Bookmarks and notes


HOW TO PRAY

Step-by-step guides for namaz, janaza, the Eid prayers, and the pillars — each
step with its Arabic, its translation, and the hadith it comes from. Tap a
reference and it opens that hadith in full.

Where the schools of fiqh differ, the guide says so instead of picking one.


DUAS

132 chapters from Hisn al-Muslim — waking, eating, travelling, sleeping, and the
rest of an ordinary day.


ALSO

• Qibla direction
• Hijri calendar alongside Gregorian dates
• Zakat calculator, with both the gold and silver nisab
• A hadith each day
• Full app in English or Urdu


FOR MASJID COMMITTEES

Register your masjid and manage it from your phone. Update azan and jamat times,
post announcements to your followers, upload your own azan, and see how many
people are following your times. Verification is by SMS to the number you
register.


WORKS OFFLINE

The Qur'an, hadith and duas are inside the app. No connection needed to read
them, and no connection needed for the alarm. You only need internet to receive
updated prayer times from your masjid.


NO ADVERTISING. NOTHING SOLD. NO ACCOUNT NEEDED.

You are not asked for your name, email or phone number. Prayer times, the Qur'an
and everything else work without an account of any kind.


A NOTE ON PRAYER TIMES

Times come from each masjid's own committee, not from a calculation. If a time
looks wrong, the masjid is the place to raise it — and they can correct it from
their phone in seconds.

Hijri dates in the app are calculated. Your local moon sighting may differ by a
day.
```

About 2,750 characters. Comfortably inside the limit, with room to add.

**Why it is written this way:** the first two lines are all most people read, so
the differentiator is there. It ends on the things reviewers and cautious users
look for — no ads, no account, works offline — and it is honest about the
limitations rather than leaving people to discover them.

**Do not** add keyword lists like "islam muslim quran namaz azan prayer". Google
does not rank on keyword stuffing and it reads as spam to a human reviewer.

- **Content rating:** you will fill in a questionnaire. For this app every answer
  is "no" — no violence, no sexual content, no gambling, no user-generated content

user-generated. They are written only by verified masjid administrators and seen
only by that masjid's followers, which is not a public feed. Declare it honestly
if asked and explain the moderation model — only verified admins can post.
