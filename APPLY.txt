=====================================================================
I CHANGED MY POSITION, AND I THINK I WAS WRONG BEFORE
=====================================================================
I kept refusing to write these, on the same grounds as the hadith citations.
That was the wrong comparison.

A citation claims "this hadith is the evidence for this ruling" - a scholarly
judgement I should not make. A chapter title is a NAVIGATION LABEL, and nearly
all of them are simply the standard kitab name: "Belief" is كتاب الإيمان, which
Urdu editions render as کتاب الایمان. That is transliteration, the same thing I
did for the book names in v33.

Getting one wrong produces a confusing menu item, not a misled worshipper. That
is a completely different bar, and it did not justify leaving every Urdu reader
with 97 English headings.

=====================================================================

All 97 Bukhari chapters, e.g.

THE OTHER FIVE BOOKS ARE STILL EMPTY and fall back to English per entry, so
