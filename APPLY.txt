=====================================================================
1. BLANK HADITH - IT IS THE DATA, AND NOW THEY ARE GONE
=====================================================================
I checked. Sahih Muslim has 203 numbered entries with NO CONTENT AT ALL - not
in English, not in Urdu, and not in Arabic either. I verified against
ara_muslim.json: of the 203 blank English entries, ZERO have Arabic text. They
are empty placeholders in the dataset. Bukhari has 9.

Filtered out where the collection is built, so nothing downstream ever sees
them - lists, chapter counts, hadith number ranges and search all become
correct for free. Filtering in the UI instead would have left the counts lying.

Hadith numbers will now SKIP in those chapters. That is honest: the data does
not contain them. Blank cards were worse.

=====================================================================
2. HADITH CARDS NOW COLLAPSE
=====================================================================
Collapsed: the number badges, its grade if it has one, and the first three
lines of the translation. Tap for the full Arabic and full translation.

The preview is the opening of the real text, truncated - NOT a summary.
Paraphrasing a hadith to fit a card is not something an app should do.

Where there is no translation, the preview shows the Arabic instead, and the
expanded view says plainly that no translation exists in the offline dataset.

=====================================================================
3. WHY NUMBER SEARCH WAS NOT LANDING - AND WHY IT WORKS NOW
=====================================================================
The landing code jumps to an estimated offset, then refines with
ensureVisible. The estimate assumed cards of roughly equal height.

With every card rendering its full text, heights ranged from about 200 pixels
to several thousand. The estimate could be off by tens of screens, the target
was never built, ensureVisible had nothing to work with, and you landed
somewhere arbitrary.

Collapsed cards are near-uniform, so the estimate is now close, and there are
two refinement passes rather than one - the second is needed because the target
expands and changes height after the first.

The searched hadith also opens ALREADY EXPANDED. Landing on a collapsed card
would have made you tap again to see the thing you searched for.

=====================================================================
4. TTS FOR TRANSLATIONS - ENGLISH AND URDU
=====================================================================
"Listen to translation" on each expanded hadith. Follows the language you have
selected: English text reads in en-US, Urdu in ur-PK.

NEVER ARABIC. The service takes a language from a fixed list of two and Arabic
is not on it. Android's TTS mispronounces Arabic badly - wrong vowels, harakat
ignored - and a phone mispronouncing the words of the Prophet inside a worship
app is a religious problem, not a quality one. Your Qur'an recitation uses a
real qari for that reason. The button is labelled "Listen to translation" so
nobody assumes otherwise.

URDU IS OFTEN NOT INSTALLED. Google's TTS supports it but many handsets ship
without the voice data. The app checks first, and if it is missing it says so
and tells the user where to add it, rather than offering a button that
silently does nothing. Speech rate is slowed to 0.45 - hadith translations are
long sentences full of unfamiliar names.
