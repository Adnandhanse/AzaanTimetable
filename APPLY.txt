Overwrite all three. Nothing else changes.

=====================================================================
WHAT CHANGED
=====================================================================
Hadith open in Urdu by default instead of English.

The choice is also STORED now. Someone who prefers English switches once and
keeps it - previously the menu reset to English on every visit, so an Urdu
reader had to re-select it constantly.

Citations in the Ibadat section follow the same preference. HadithLink had
English hardcoded, so tapping "Bukhari 735" under a namaz step would have opened
in English while the rest of the app was in Urdu.

=====================================================================
URDU COVERAGE - CHECKED, AND ONE BOOK IS THIN
=====================================================================
Counted from your own files, after the blank-entry filter:

  Abu Dawud    5274 shown, 5273 have Urdu text   (100%)
  Ibn Majah    4340 shown, 4329 have Urdu text   (100%)
  Muslim       7482 shown, 7459 have Urdu text   (100%)
  Bukhari      7583 shown, 7019 have Urdu text   ( 93%)
  NASA'I       5683 shown, 3874 have Urdu text   ( 68%)

So roughly a third of Sunan an-Nasa'i has Arabic but NO Urdu translation, and
about 7% of Bukhari. Those hadith are not blank - the Arabic is there - so they
still appear, showing the Arabic with a plain note that no translation exists in
the offline dataset. Nothing silently disappears.

If that gap in Nasa'i matters to you, the fix is a better Urdu dataset for that
book, not code. Worth knowing before users find it.
