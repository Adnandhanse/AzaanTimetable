=====================================================================
1. JAMAT COLUMNS WERE CLUTTERED
=====================================================================
I had used two fixed 66px boxes plus a Spacer plus an Expanded label plus the
Arabic name. On a 360dp screen the name and the Arabic were left fighting over
whatever remained, and nothing lined up.

Everything is FLEX now, no fixed pixel widths anywhere: 4 : 2 : 3 : 3 for name,
Arabic, azan, jamat. It scales with the screen instead of assuming one. The
column headings use the same flex values, so they cannot drift out of alignment
with the numbers underneath.

The Arabic name also drops from 16 to 14pt and the label tracking tightens when
two time columns are present — small, but it is the difference between tight and
cramped.

=====================================================================
2. ANNOUNCEMENT - THIRD ATTEMPT, AND THIS TIME NOT A BOX
=====================================================================
The first was a bordered white card. The second was a gold-tinted strip with an
accent bar. You said both looked like a box stuck on the page, and you were
right both times — ANY filled rectangle reads as a panel on a flat ivory layout.

So there is no fill, no border and no card. It is a line of text with a small
icon between two hairlines, and those hairlines are the same ones the prayer
list already uses. It sits in the page rather than on it.

4. THE FULL JANAZA DUA
=====================================================================
The guide had only the SHORT general dua — "forgive our living and our dead" —
which is a real narration but is not the dua for the deceased. Anyone leading a
janaza would have found the guide stopped exactly where they needed it.

Now the complete dua from SAHIH MUSLIM 2232, with English and Urdu:
"O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him... give him an abode better than his
abode... admit him to the Garden..."

Verified verbatim against ara_muslim.json — and one detail worth knowing:

THE SOURCE READS أَوْ, NOT وَ. "...from the punishment of the grave OR the
punishment of the Fire." That is the narrator's uncertainty about which the
Prophet ﷺ said. I first wrote "and", which is how many printed versions render
it, and the verbatim check caught it. Kept as أَوْ — smoothing it would be me
correcting a narration to read better.

Also added a note that the dua is worded for a male deceased and the pronouns
change for a female.

5. FULL PRAYER SCHEDULE NOW SHOWS JAMAT
=====================================================================
It was azan only. Opening "View full prayer schedule" from a home screen that
already showed both columns gave you LESS information, which makes the fuller

Rebuilt with the same flex proportions as the home list, so the two read as one
