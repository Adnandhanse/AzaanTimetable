THE FONT LICENCE - YOU WERE RIGHT, I WAS WRONG
=====================================================================
I said twice that the KFGQPC package had no licence. It does. It is embedded in
the font's own name table, not shipped as a separate file, and I only looked for a
file. My mistake, and I pressed the point harder than the evidence supported.

The terms are good for you. Verbatim:

    "Permission is hereby granted, Free of Cost, to any person obtaining a copy
     of this Font ... the rights to Use, Copy, Distribute"

No non-commercial restriction. Distribution is explicitly allowed, which is what
bundling it in an app is.

ONE CONDITION THAT MATTERS: the font may not be "Sold, Modified, Altered,
Translated, Reverse Engineered, Decompiled". So:

  - Ship the .otf exactly as issued. I verified the file in your repo is
    BYTE-IDENTICAL to the one you sent — same MD5, 43269f11...
  - Do not enable font subsetting. Flutter's tree-shaking only touches ICON
    fonts, not bundled text fonts, so your build is fine as it stands. If anyone
    ever adds subsetting to shrink the APK, that would breach this licence.

DATA SAFETY - THE ANSWERS, VERIFIED AGAINST THE CODE
=====================================================================
Declare exactly THREE data types:

  Phone number            collected, not shared, stored, OPTIONAL,
                          purpose: account management
  Approximate location    collected, not shared, PROCESSED EPHEMERALLY,
                          optional, purpose: app functionality
  User-generated content  collected, not shared, optional,
                          purpose: app functionality

And nothing else. I checked each "not collected" claim against the code:
  - zakat_service.dart makes NO network calls, so "financial info" is genuinely
    not collected
  - there is no Crashlytics and no Analytics in pubspec.yaml, so "diagnostics" is
    genuinely not collected
  - the follower and daily-active counters are aggregates with no user data, so
    they need no declaration

THE ONE THAT CATCHES PEOPLE: location must be ticked "processed ephemerally",
because your app reads the position and compares it on the device without ever
uploading it. Your privacy policy says exactly that. Those two statements have to
agree, and they do.

Your manifest requests both COARSE and FINE location. Declare APPROXIMATE, since
that is what a 7 km radius and a qibla bearing actually need. If you would rather
form and manifest match perfectly, say so and I will drop ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION.
