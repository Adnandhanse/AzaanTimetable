=====================================================================
FOLLOWER COUNT
=====================================================================
Every masjid document gets a followerCount, maintained by atomic increments.
Changing masjid decrements the old and increments the new in ONE BATCH, so the
two cannot drift apart because one write landed and the other did not.

Shown in two places:
  Admin dashboard   "12 followers" under each masjid the admin manages
  Masjid search     "... • 12 following", hidden at zero so a newly registered
                    masjid does not advertise an empty number

The count is hooked into UserRepository.setSelectedMasjid rather than each call
site, so no future caller can forget it.

=====================================================================
DAILY ACTIVE COUNT - AND WHY IT IS NOT A PER-USER LAST LOGIN
=====================================================================
One document per date: stats/daily_2026-08-05 with activeDevices, incremented
once per device per day.

You asked to save each user's last login. I have not, and I think that is the
right call for two reasons.

FIRST, IT CANNOT DO WHAT YOU WANTED IT FOR. You said the purpose was to follow
up with people who stopped opening the app. Your users are anonymous Firebase
accounts - no name, no phone, no email. There is nobody to contact. A per-user
timestamp would tell you someone disappeared and leave you unable to do anything
about it. Making follow-up possible means collecting contact details, which is
the signup wall we agreed against.

SECOND, THE COUNT ANSWERS THE REAL QUESTION WITHOUT THE COST. "How many people
use the app daily" is a number. A counter gives you daily and monthly actives,
retention trends, and the effect of a release - with no user records at all.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT STORED ANYWHERE:
  no names, phones or emails for worshippers
  no device identifiers
  no list of who follows which masjid
  no per-user last-seen timestamp

Nothing here can identify anyone, and nothing here could reveal an individual's
prayer habits if the database leaked. That keeps you clear of consent, purpose
limitation, deletion-on-request and breach notification for this data.

=====================================================================
THE TRADEOFF, STATED PLAINLY
=====================================================================
COUNTS DRIFT. A reinstall counts as a new follower, because the app keeps
nothing that would recognise a returning device. Treat the numbers as
indicative, not exact. Making them exact would mean identifying devices, which
is a bad trade for a prayer app.

Offline changes are skipped rather than retried, on purpose - a missed increment
costs one off a count, a double increment inflates it permanently.

=====================================================================
FIRESTORE RULES - YOU MUST UPDATE THESE
=====================================================================
Two things need permission:
  masjids/{id}   increment of followerCount by any signed-in user
  stats/{doc}    create and increment by any signed-in user

If your rules do not allow them, both writes fail silently - by design, so
analytics can never stop the app working. You would see counts stay at zero.

STILL OUTSTANDING: you collect admin phone numbers, so a privacy policy is
required before Play Store submission regardless of any of the above.
