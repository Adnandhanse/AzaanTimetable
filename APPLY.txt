JAMAT TIMES
=====================================================================
ADMIN: each prayer is now one row with two taps - Azan and Jamat side by side.
Not two separate lists: an admin setting Isha thinks "azan 7:40, jamat 8:00" as
one decision, and two lists would mean scrolling between them and mismatching a
pair.

Jamat shows "Optional" rather than "Not set", so it is clear that leaving it
blank is allowed.

USER: the prayer list gains a second column, with AZAN and JAMAT headings.

  FAJR    الفجر     5:12    5:30
  DHUHR   الظهر    12:40    1:00

Azan is in the lighter weight because it is what the alarm fires on; JAMAT is in
the stronger one because it is what people plan around. A prayer with no jamat
set shows a dash so the column stays aligned.

THE HEADINGS AND THE SECOND COLUMN ONLY APPEAR IF THE MASJID HAS SET AT LEAST
ONE JAMAT TIME. Every masjid registered before today has none, and would
otherwise show an empty column under a heading, looking broken.

NO NOTIFICATIONS ON JAMAT, as you asked. I checked: nothing in
notification_service, foreground_alarm_service, foreground_alarm_manager or
prayer_schedule_cache reads these fields. Alarms still fire on the azan only.

MIGRATION IS AUTOMATIC. Missing jamat fields read as empty, so existing masjids
keep working untouched and simply do not show the column until their admin fills
it in.
