=====================================================================
BE CLEAR ABOUT WHAT THIS ACHIEVES
=====================================================================
100% is not reachable on Android, by anyone. If the user swipes the app out of
recents on a Xiaomi, or the system force-stops it, Android CANCELS every alarm
the app registered. No third-party app survives that - Google's own Clock is
whitelisted by name.

What this gets you is as reliable as Android permits, roughly 95-99% once the
user grants three things, plus the app now noticing and repairing its own
failures instead of dying quietly.

=====================================================================
1. HIGHEST-TIER SCHEDULING  (biggest single win)
=====================================================================
  AndroidScheduleMode.exactAllowWhileIdle  ->  AndroidScheduleMode.alarmClock

exactAllowWhileIdle is an exact alarm doze PERMITS. alarmClock is the primitive
Android uses for the user's own alarm clock: exempt from doze, far more
resistant to battery optimisation, the strongest scheduling call a third-party
app can make.

Tradeoff, so you are not surprised: the phone shows an alarm icon in the status
bar and reports the next prayer as the device's next alarm. For a prayer alarm
app I would argue that is correct, not a side effect.

=====================================================================
2. ALARMS NO LONGER DEPEND ON THE NETWORK
=====================================================================
  NEW  lib/services/prayer_schedule_cache.dart

Alarms were armed only when Home's Firestore stream emitted. Open the app on a
bad connection and NOTHING was scheduled.

Prayer times are now cached on the device. main() arms alarms from that cache
before Firebase or Firestore is touched. Firestore updates prayer times; it no
longer decides whether an alarm exists.

=====================================================================
3. THE APP NOW CHECKS ITS OWN ALARMS AND REPAIRS THEM
=====================================================================
  NotificationService.verifyAndRepair()

Runs at every app start and every time the app returns to the foreground. It
asks the OS what it still holds, compares against what should be there, and
re-arms the whole set if anything is missing.

Alarms disappear for reasons the app cannot see - a force-stop, an OEM cleanup,
a reboot. Previously the app scheduled and hoped, and a wipe was invisible
until someone missed a prayer. Now it self-heals on next open.

=====================================================================
4. ALARM HEALTH SCREEN  (Settings > Alarm health)
=====================================================================
  NEW  lib/screens/alarm_health_screen.dart

- Live status of every permission that matters, each with its own Fix button
- Whether the background service is actually running
- What the OS currently has scheduled, with next fire times
- TEST ALARM IN 60 SECONDS - fires down the exact same path a prayer alarm
  uses, so someone can verify on THEIR handset instead of finding out at Fajr
- Step-by-step Autostart instructions for Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO, Realme/OPPO,
  vivo/iQOO, Samsung, OnePlus and stock Android

Written out per manufacturer rather than auto-detected, deliberately - that
would have meant adding device_info_plus, and you have had enough plugin
trouble in CI.
