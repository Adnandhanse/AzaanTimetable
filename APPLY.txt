v14 - FINDING THE ALARM FAILURE
===============================

Full superset of v1-v13. Unzip over your repo root, overwriting.

*** IT INCLUDES .github/workflows/build.yml - YOU MUST APPLY THAT FILE ***
That workflow step is what copies the azan into android/app/src/main/res/raw.
If you only ever copied lib/ files, the azan has never been in your APK, and
that alone explains everything you are seeing. See below.

=====================================================================
ANSWERS
=====================================================================
Does the azan need an audio permission?  NO. Notification sound is not
permission-gated. That is not the cause.

Can we take all permissions at the start?  YES - added. First launch now
requests notifications, exact alarms and battery exemption in one pass, and
records that it asked so it does not nag.

=====================================================================
WHAT YOUR SCREENSHOT RULES OUT, AND WHAT IT POINTS AT
=====================================================================
All four checks green. Test armed on tier 1 (azan + alarmClock). And it still
did not fire. Scheduling SUCCEEDED and the alarm still produced nothing - so
the failure is AFTER scheduling, when Android tries to post the notification.

Combined with your hint - one notification, no azan, then nothing ever again -
this points hard at the NOTIFICATION CHANNEL.

The mechanism: a channel's sound is frozen when Android first creates it. If
res/raw/azan.mp3 was not in the APK at that moment, the channel was created
with a dangling sound URI, permanently. Android then DROPS every notification
posted to it, silently. App updates do not repair it, because Android refuses
to modify an existing channel.

=====================================================================
WHAT v14 DOES ABOUT IT
=====================================================================

1. CHANNEL IDS BUMPED TO v2, and every older id is now deleted on startup
   (prayer_times_channel, prayer_alarm_azan_v1, prayer_alarm_plain_v1).
   This escapes any channel that was created broken.

2. CHANNELS ARE CREATED EXPLICITLY at startup instead of implicitly by the
   first notification. Implicit creation happens once, invisibly, and whatever
   state it captures is permanent - not something to leave to chance.

3. "SHOW A NOTIFICATION RIGHT NOW" button in Alarm health.
   This bypasses AlarmManager entirely. It is the diagnostic that has been
   missing:
       nothing appears  -> the CHANNEL is broken. Alarms are irrelevant.
       it appears       -> the channel is fine; the problem is scheduling or
                           the phone freezing the app.
   Nothing else separates those two, and they need opposite fixes.

4. "USE THE BUNDLED AZAN" SWITCH in Alarm health.
   Turn it OFF and alarms move to a channel with the phone's default alert
   sound - a channel that cannot fail the dangling-URI way. If alarms start
   working with it off, the azan resource is confirmed as the cause.

=====================================================================
DO THIS, IN THIS ORDER
=====================================================================
1. Apply v14 INCLUDING .github/workflows/build.yml, and rebuild.
2. UNINSTALL the app from the phone first, then install the new APK.
   Not an upgrade - an uninstall. Channels survive upgrades, and a broken one
   has to be removed with the app.
3. Settings > Alarm health > "Show a notification RIGHT NOW".
      appears with azan     -> fixed, go to step 4
      appears, wrong sound  -> azan not in the APK; check the workflow ran
      nothing at all        -> tell me, that is a different fault
4. Turn the azan switch OFF and press it again. If it appears now, the azan
   resource is the problem.
5. Then the 60-second test.

Also: your status bar showed the ringer muted. Check media/alarm volume and
whether Do Not Disturb is on before concluding anything.
