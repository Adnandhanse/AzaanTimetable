THE IFTAR DUA - THE TEXT CHANGED, NOT JUST THE CITATION
=====================================================================
You approved Abu Dawud 2357. That hadith does NOT contain the dua you originally
supplied. It contains a different one, so I used ITS wording:

    ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الْأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ
    "Thirst has gone, the arteries are moist, and the reward is sure, if Allah
     wills."

Putting 2357 beside "Allahumma inni laka sumtu" would have been citing a hadith
that does not contain those words. If you wanted your original wording kept, say
so - it ships uncited, like the sehri dua.
