past the whole thing to start listening - the one thing you want before reading,
not after.

2. ADMIN TAB NOW ADMINS ONLY
=====================================================================
Four tabs for users, five for admins. Read from the role saved at first launch.

You are right that showing an admin login to someone who just said they are not
an admin makes the question pointless. Changeable any time from Settings > How
you use the app.

=====================================================================
3. SETTINGS CLEANED UP
=====================================================================
REMOVED: Send Test Notification Now, Show My Account ID, Check Scheduled Alarms.
All three were labelled "Debug:" in the UI. Three of eight rows saying "Debug"
tells a user this is unfinished software.

ALARM HEALTH SLIMMED. Removed: the 60-second and instant test buttons, the event
log and its Copy button, the "Last scheduling run" panel, "Re-arm alarms now",
the list of scheduled alarms, and the azan-sound and alarm-clock toggles.

Those toggles were the ones I most wanted gone: they let a user break their own
alarms by flipping a setting whose consequences they cannot judge.

WHAT REMAINS is what someone can act on - which permissions are missing, a Fix
button for each, whether the background service is running, and the
manufacturer-specific steps for the one thing no app can set for them.

The service code behind all of it is untouched, so anything can be restored by
putting the widget back.

ONE THING I WOULD ARGUE FOR KEEPING, though I removed it as asked: the
60-second test. Users of prayer apps genuinely want to confirm alarms work on
their own phone rather than trusting it until they miss Fajr. Say the word and I
will put that one back on its own.

=====================================================================
4. DUAS - IT WAS ALREADY BUILT, AND BROKEN
=====================================================================
DuaHomeScreen, DuaDetailScreen, DuaRepository and the Dua model all already
existed in your codebase. But:

    - NOTHING navigated to any of them
    - assets/duas/duas_en.json WAS NEVER DECLARED in pubspec.yaml

So the feature was unreachable, and would have thrown on
rootBundle.loadString the moment anything did reach it.

Both fixed. The asset is declared and a Duas card now sits in the Ibadat tab,
beside Umrah in the "Also" section - neither is a pillar.

Your data: 132 chapters from Hisn al-Muslim, each with Arabic, English
translation, a repeat count, and an audio URL.

=====================================================================
URDU DUAS - YOU ASKED, AND THE ANSWER IS NO
=====================================================================
duas_en.json has ARABIC_TEXT and TRANSLATED_TEXT. The translation is ENGLISH
ONLY. There is no Urdu field, and no Urdu file in the project.

So with the app set to Urdu, dua translations will still read in English. Options:

  1. Leave English, and let TTS read it - already wired for hadith
  2. Source an Urdu Hisn al-Muslim dataset. They exist; check the licence before
     bundling one, same as with the hadith data
  3. Have the 132 chapters translated - that is a real translation job, not a
     transliteration like the chapter titles, and not something I should invent

I would not machine-translate duas. Unlike a menu label, a wrong dua translation
misleads someone in worship.
