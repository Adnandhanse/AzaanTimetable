1. MARK YOUR PLACE WHILE READING ARABIC-ONLY
   lib/widgets/mushaf_view.dart

   You were right - the bookmark only existed on translation verse rows, so in
   Arabic-only mode there was no way to save where you were.

   The footer now reads:

     Page 3 of 12  ·  Ayah 45-58  ·  15 lines        [Save page]

   Tap "Save page" and the first ayah on that page becomes your saved position.
   It flips to "Saved" when your saved verse is on the page you are looking at.

   Making this work needed a change under the hood. The view flows all the
   verses into one string to paginate them, which normally throws away where
   each verse begins. It now records every verse's character offset and does a
   binary search to work out which verses a page holds. Without that, a page
   could not tell you what was on it, and marking from this view would have
   been impossible.

   The Juz screen shows no Save button. A juz spans several surahs, so a bare
   verse number there is ambiguous - there is no single surah to attribute it
   to. Say the word if you want juz positions saved too; it needs the surah
   stored alongside the verse.

2. ARABIC-ONLY IS NOW THE DEFAULT
   Surahs and juz open in Arabic only. Translation is a button away, not the
   starting point.

   The choice is remembered. Turn translation on and it stays on until you turn
   it off - nobody re-toggles it every time they open a surah. Surah and Juz
   share the one preference, so setting it in either place sets it for both.

3. CONTINUE READING CARD RESTYLED
   Off the solid green. It is now a white card with a gold hairline, a gold
   medallion and emerald type - the same construction as every other card in
   the app.

   The green slab read as a banner from a different product. A card that
   belongs to the palette gets noticed by the medallion, not by shouting.
