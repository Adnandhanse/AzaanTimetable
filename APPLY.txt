=====================================================================
THIS CLIP IS A MUCH BETTER FIT, AND HERE IS WHY
=====================================================================
The old one was LANDSCAPE, 848x478. Filling a portrait phone with it meant
cropping about 75% OF ITS WIDTH — you were seeing a narrow centre strip and
nothing else. It only looked acceptable because the Kaaba happened to be dead
centre.

The new one is PORTRAIT, 478x850. On the same phones the crop is under 20%, so
the framing you shot survives.

  old  848x478  ->  S22 Ultra: 74% of the width cropped
  new  478x850  ->  S22 Ultra: 17% of the width cropped

=====================================================================
TWO THINGS TO KNOW
=====================================================================
IT IS 5 SECONDS AND THE SPLASH LASTS 3. The video gets cut off rather than
looping, which is better than a visible restart mid-splash. If you want the
whole clip seen, the splash delay in splash_screen.dart is one number — but a
5-second wait before the app opens is a long time every single launch, and I
would leave it at 3.
