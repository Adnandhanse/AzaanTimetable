=====================================================================
1. THE MISSING TEXT ON THE IMAGE - MY BUG
=====================================================================
Your screenshot shows the photograph with no masjid name, no address, no
directions button. They were being rendered the entire time. They were white
text over the brightest part of that picture - pale marble and a golden sunset -
with only a gentle scrim behind them.

I tuned that scrim against the NIGHT sky, where white on dark needs almost
nothing. On a bright masjid photo it did nothing at all, and the text was white
on near-white.

Fixed: the scrim is now near-opaque at the top and fades out by two thirds down.
A scrim that has to work at BOTH ends of the range has to be strong enough for
the brightest case. The picture loses a strip at the top; the text becomes
readable on every masjid photo instead of only the dark ones. That is the right
trade.

=====================================================================
4. QURAN IMAGE CUT OFF
=====================================================================
The box was a fixed 196px tall with BoxFit.cover. The artwork is 1.702:1; the box
on a typical phone is about 1.84:1. Cover fills the width, overflows vertically,
and crops the top and bottom - the lanterns and the base of the stand.

Now wrapped in AspectRatio(1.70), so the box is the same shape as the picture.
Nothing to crop, nothing letterboxed, and the height follows screen width instead
of being guessed:

    360dp wide -> 212dp tall     390dp -> 229dp     412dp -> 242dp

=====================================================================
5. NEW - ROLE SELECTION AT FIRST LAUNCH
=====================================================================
    Language  ->  Role  ->  the app

  "I am here to pray"        -> Home. Follow a masjid, azan alarm, Quran,
                                Hadith, Qibla.
  "I manage a masjid"        -> Admin login, from where a masjid is registered.

Role is asked AFTER language, so the two choices are not both presented in a
language the user may not read. Asked once; the saved answer decides where they
land afterwards.

IT IS NOT A LOCK. Both routes stay reachable - the Masjid Admin tab is still
there for users, and admins still get the full app. Someone can be both, and a
mistaken tap here should not shut anyone out of half the app. The screen says so.

Note: the admin route goes to AdminLoginScreen, which is where masjid
registration is reached from. If you would rather it open the registration form
directly for a first-time admin, that is a one-line change - tell me which you
prefer.
