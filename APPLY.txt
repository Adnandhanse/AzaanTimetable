=====================================================================
1. SEHRI DUA REMOVED
=====================================================================
Gone. It is in none of the six books and there was nothing to cite.

The step is still there, because sahur itself IS established - it now describes
the practice and says the niyyah is an intention of the heart, not a fixed
formula. Cited to Bukhari 1923, "take suhur, there is a blessing in it", which
is the hadith that actually supports the step.

=====================================================================
2. ZAKAT TOTALS MOVED UNDER EACH METAL
=====================================================================
    Gold
      grams, karat
      Value 320,833.33          Zakat 2.5%  8,020.83

    Silver
      grams, purity
      Value 44,955.00           Zakat 2.5%  1,123.88

    Cash / Savings
      amount, liabilities
      Value 100,000.00          Zakat 2.5%  2,500.00

Previously all of it sat in one card at the bottom, so you entered gold grams at
the top and found the gold value four sections away. Under the inputs, the number
answers the question at the moment it is asked.

The bottom card is now the summary only - total, less liabilities, net, nisab,
and the verdict. Repeating the per-holding figures there would have printed the
same numbers twice, and the second copy is exactly where someone would start
adding them up by hand. The note about zakat being due on the net total sits with
the final amount.

A section shows nothing until you enter something in it.

=====================================================================
3. GET DIRECTIONS ON THE IMAGE
=====================================================================
A translucent pill under the masjid name and address, on the photograph.

It has to stay readable over a picture whose brightness swings from midday sky to
near-black, so it is a dark translucent fill with a white hairline rather than
plain text. Hidden entirely when the masjid has no coordinates, rather than
opening an empty map.

=====================================================================
Name, address, date and directions are all on the image now, so the card below
was the same information a second time. Removed, along with the builder for it.

"View full prayer schedule" stays.

NOTE: the verified / pending-verification badge lived in that card and is now
gone from Home. It is still on the masjid search screen. Say if you want it back
somewhere.

=====================================================================
