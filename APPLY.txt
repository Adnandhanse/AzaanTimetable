=====================================================================
THREE THINGS WERE WRONG, AND ONLY ONE OF THEM WAS THE STYLING
=====================================================================
1. THE CARDS WERE HALF EMPTY.
   childAspectRatio was 0.74, giving a 228pt card for about 183pt of content —
   45pt of dead space across the bottom of every one. That empty band is the
   single thing that made this screen look unfinished, and it is why the design
   read as weak rather than minimal.

   Now 0.86, which is 197pt. Measured, not guessed: medallion 36 + 10 + a 38pt
   name block + Arabic 22 + rule + two lines of counts, inside 26pt of padding.
   14pt spare.

2. FIVE OF THE SIX NAMES WRAP TO TWO LINES.
   "Sahih al-Bukhari", "Sunan Abu Dawud", "Jami' at-Tirmidhi", "Sunan an-Nasa'i",
   "Sunan Ibn Majah" all wrap; only "Sahih Muslim" fits on one. So in that one
   card the Arabic, the rule and the volume count all sat 18pt higher than in the
   other five — which is exactly the sort of misalignment you notice without
   being able to name.

   The name now occupies a FIXED 38pt block with the text centred inside it. All
   six cards line up whatever the text does.

3. THE CARDS SAID ALMOST NOTHING.
   "9 volumes" describes a printed edition somebody else published. It tells you
   nothing about what is in your hand.

   Added the hadith count, in emerald above the volume line:

       Sahih al-Bukhari    7,580 hadith    9 volumes
       Sahih Muslim        7,360 hadith    7 volumes
       Sunan Abu Dawud     5,272 hadith    4 volumes
       Jami' at-Tirmidhi   3,926 hadith    6 volumes
       Sunan an-Nasa'i     5,679 hadith    6 volumes
       Sunan Ibn Majah     4,340 hadith    5 volumes

=====================================================================
THOSE COUNTS ARE THE APP'S OWN, NOT THE PUBLISHED TOTALS
=====================================================================
I counted entries WITH TEXT in your data files, not the nominal totals.

Sahih Muslim's file holds 7,563 entries but 203 are empty and the app already
filters them out before display. Printing 7,563 on the card would be a number the
app itself contradicts the moment somebody scrolls to the end.

Same for the others: Bukhari 7,589 nominal / 7,580 shown, Tirmidhi 3,998 / 3,926,
Nasa'i 5,765 / 5,679, Ibn Majah 4,343 / 4,340, Abu Dawud 5,274 / 5,272.

If you would rather show the published figures, say so — but then the two numbers
disagree, and I would not.
