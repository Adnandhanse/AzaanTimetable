=====================================================================
1. SPLASH VIDEO NOW FILLS THE SCREEN
=====================================================================
It was BoxFit.contain, which fits a whole 16:9 clip inside a 9:20 phone and
leaves two large green bands - exactly your screenshot.

Now BoxFit.cover: scales until both dimensions are filled, cropping the sides.

WORTH KNOWING: this only looks right because the Kaaba is centred in your clip.
A video with anything important near the left or right edge would lose it. If
you swap the clip later and it looks wrong, that is why.

=====================================================================
2. DAILY HADITH - TRANSLUCENT, SHORT, AND BILINGUAL
=====================================================================
TRANSLUCENT: 92% opacity, so the app stays visible behind it. The first version
was a tall opaque card that covered the home screen and read as an interruption
rather than something laid on top.

SHORT: the saying only, chain of narrators removed. Two or three lines. Nobody
reads a paragraph on a card that appeared uninvited.

To be precise about what that means: the text is still VERBATIM from your data -
the quoted words, with the isnad dropped. Nothing paraphrased, nothing shortened
mid-sentence. Where a hadith has several clauses, one complete clause is shown
and "Read in full" gives the rest. Bukhari 6136 is the one that applies to: the
card shows the clause about speech, the full hadith also covers neighbours and
guests.

URDU: yes. Every one of the ten is in your urd_*.json files, so the card follows
the app language - Urdu text, right-aligned, RTL, with an Urdu theme word
(نیت، رحم، اخلاق...). English otherwise. Your Urdu translations, not mine.

=====================================================================
3. ANNOUNCEMENT NO LONGER LOOKS LIKE A BOX ON THE PAGE
=====================================================================
It was a bordered white card on an ivory background - a box stuck on top,
exactly as you said.

Now a soft gold wash with a single accent bar down the leading edge and no
outline, so it reads as part of the page that happens to be highlighted. One
strong vertical line draws the eye without drawing a rectangle.

Also dropped the repeated masjid name from each strip - you are already looking
at that masjid's home screen.

=====================================================================
4. QURAN - PAGE BAR AND BOOKMARK MOVED TO THE TOP
=====================================================================
The page number, ayah range and the Save/bookmark control were UNDER the text.
On a full page of Arabic that put them below the fold: you had to read to the
end before you could see where you were or save your place. Both are now at the
top, where the eye starts.

DIVIDER LINES REMOVED, three of them - the two on the Qur'an home screen and the
one under the page bar. The artwork already ends on a hard edge, the tab bar has
its own indicator, and the page is a bordered card. Every one of those rules was
drawn between two things that were already separated.
