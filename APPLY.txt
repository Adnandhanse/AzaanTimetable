UI CHANGES v7 - 48 files
========================

Full superset of v1-v6.1. Unzip over your repo root, overwriting.

1. JUZ NOW HAS BOTH READING MODES
   lib/screens/juz_detail_screen.dart, lib/widgets/mushaf_view.dart

   The Arabic-only / Translation toggle existed on the surah screen only. It is
   now on Juz too, same control in the same place.

   To do it, MushafView was changed to take a plain LIST OF VERSES instead of a
   Surah. A juz spans several surahs, so a Surah-shaped API could never have
   worked for it. Surah screen updated for the new signature.

   The Juz screen also picks up proper theming: verse references in emerald,
   gold hairline dividers, surah headers with the Arabic name in gold.

2. CONTINUE READING
   lib/services/quran_local_data_service.dart  - storage
   lib/screens/surah_detail_screen.dart        - tracking + resume
   lib/screens/quran_home_screen.dart          - the card

   A green "CONTINUE READING" card sits at the top of the Quran tab showing
   the surah, and the ayah when you have marked one. Tapping it reopens the
   surah exactly where you were.

   TWO LEVELS OF PRECISION, BOTH SAVED:
     - Scroll position, saved automatically as you read (throttled to once
       every 2 seconds - writing on every scroll frame would hammer the disk),
       and once more when you leave the screen.
     - An exact verse, when you tap the new bookmark icon on any verse.

   Why both: a scroll offset stops meaning anything if the font size or the
   device changes. A verse number always survives. The offset is the nicety;
   the verse is the guarantee. Auto-saves deliberately do NOT overwrite a verse
   you marked by hand in the same surah.

   Only ONE position is kept. A reading app that remembers thirty places
   remembers none of them usefully.

3. NAME, ADDRESS AND DATE MOVED ONTO THE IMAGE
   lib/widgets/artwork_header.dart, lib/screens/home_screen.dart

   Header is now 300px, up from a 200px picture under a ~90px white bar. The
   photograph gets all that space back.

   Text sits top-left over a gradient scrim. The scrim is not decoration: the
   sky swings from pale midday blue to near-black at night, and white text
   would disappear against the bright states without it. Type also carries a
   soft shadow as a second line of defence.

   Address is included, on its own line above the date - most masjid addresses
   are too long to sit beside a date on one line.

   NOTE: this is the third arrangement we have tried, and it reverses the v5
   change. Originally the name sat over the middle of the picture, across the
   dome, and was unreadable. Top-left over a scrim is a different thing from
   that, and it buys back the height. If it still reads badly on a bright
   midday sky, the scrim stops are one line to deepen.
