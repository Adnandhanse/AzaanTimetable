=====================================================================
THE BUG
=====================================================================
Choosing "I manage a masjid" used pushReplacement, which REPLACED the role
screen with the admin login. Nothing was left beneath it, so back exited the app.

Anyone who tapped the wrong card had to kill the app and reopen it - and on
reopening, the saved role sent them straight back to the admin login. A dead end
they could not get out of without reinstalling.

=====================================================================THREE FIXES
=====================================================================
1. THE ADMIN ROUTE IS NOW PUSHED, NOT REPLACED
   The role screen stays underneath, so the admin login's AppBar shows a real
   back arrow and back returns to the choice - the page you asked for.

2. A SAVED ADMIN ROLE LANDS ON HOME WITH THE LOGIN ON TOP
   On later launches the role screen is not in play, so Home becomes the root and
   the admin login sits above it. Back goes to Home rather than out of the app.
   Admins still start in the admin flow.

3. THE ROLE CHOICE IS CHANGEABLE
   Settings > "How you use the app" reopens it. Asked once at first launch;
   without this, picking wrong could only be undone by reinstalling.

   Also added "Skip for now" on the role screen itself, so that screen is not a
   dead end either.
