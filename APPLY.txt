NEW IN v5.1 - lib/screens/hadith_chapters_screen.dart
=====================================================

1. HADITH NUMBER RANGE UNDER EVERY CHAPTER
   Each chapter row now reads, for example:

     2   Belief
         Hadith 8-58  ·  51 total

   Verified against your own eng_bukhari.json:
     Revelation  1-7      (7)
     Belief      8-58     (51)
     Knowledge   59-134   (76)
     Prayers     349-520  (167)

   Note your example said "believe 222-385". The real range in your dataset is
   8-58 - your data uses international hadith numbering, and that is what the
   app will show. If you wanted the Arabic-edition numbering instead, say so;
   the field is in the data and it is a small change.

   Ranges are computed once when the book loads, not per row. Computing them
   inside the list builder would rescan all 7000+ hadith on every frame of
   every scroll.

2. SEARCH BY HADITH NUMBER
   The filter box now takes either a chapter name or a number.
   Type 300 and you get the chapter CONTAINING hadith 300 - not the chapter
   numbered 300, which is the less useful reading. A chapter whose own number
   matches also shows. A confirmation line appears above the results, and it
   tells you plainly when no chapter contains that number.

   The box is also restyled to match the rest of the app - it was still using
   the bare Material default, which is part of why that screen looked foreign.
