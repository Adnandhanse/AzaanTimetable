DAILY HADITH "READ MORE" - ENDLESS LOADING
=====================================================================
A real bug, and a subtle one.

The button called Navigator.pop() to close the card, then handed HadithLink the
DIALOG'S context. By then that route was gone. HadithLink opens a blocking
spinner on the context it is given — so the spinner went up on a dead route and
nothing could ever take it down.

Fixed by inverting it: the dialog now POPS WITH the reference it wants opened,
and the screen that opened the dialog — whose context is alive — does the
navigating.

=====================================================================
PLAYBACK SPEED - HADITH AND QUR'AN
=====================================================================
Four speeds, 0.75x / 1x / 1.25x / 1.5x, in three places:

  hadith list        beside "Listen to translation"
  single hadith      under the listen button
  Qur'an reader      a bar at the bottom, ONLY while a recitation is playing

ONE WIDGET, NOT TWO. The two features need different numbers underneath — the
TTS engine takes an absolute rate where 0.45 is normal, audioplayers takes a
multiplier where 1.0 is normal — but the reader sees the same four labels. Two
different controls for the same idea is how a small app starts feeling
inconsistent.

THE TTS RATE IS REMEMBERED across launches and re-applied on every utterance, so
changing it mid-listen takes effect on the next play rather than the next app
start. The recitation rate is per-session, because it is set while listening.

CHIPS, NOT A SLIDER. A slider invites fiddling and lands people on rates that are
unintelligible, and nobody can hear the difference between 1.12x and 1.18x.

The Qur'an bar only appears while audio is playing — permanently visible it would
be clutter on a reading screen.
