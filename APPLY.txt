=====================================================================
THE "BLUISH" COLOUR - FOUND IT, AND IT WAS ONE THING
=====================================================================

Your screenshots 2, 3 and 4 all show the same pale blue-green card. Cause:

  ~20 screens use a bare Card() widget. Material 3 IGNORES
  ThemeData.cardColor. Card, Dialog and BottomSheet take their background
  from colorScheme.surfaceContainerLow, which Flutter derives from the SEED
  colour's tonal palette. A green seed yields a desaturated blue-green.

That is the blue. It was never a font or a screen - it was the theme, on every
screen that uses a Card. Fixed centrally in app_theme.dart:

  - surfaceContainerLowest/Low  -> white
  - surfaceContainer/High       -> cream
  - surfaceContainerHighest     -> ivory
  - surfaceTint                 -> transparent (stops elevation re-tinting)
  - cardTheme                   -> white, flat, gold hairline, 4px corners
  - dialogTheme, inputDecorationTheme, listTileTheme to match

One change, every screen. Admin panel, hadith cards and surah verse cards all
go white with a gold hairline. Do not delete those surfaceContainer lines to
"tidy up" the theme - the tint comes straight back.

NEW - TWO WAYS TO READ A SURAH
=====================================================================

A toggle in the surah screen's app bar switches between:

  TRANSLATION   verse by verse, Arabic + translation, audio, notes (as now)
  ARABIC ONLY   continuous flowing text, paginated, verse-end markers with
                Arabic-Indic numerals, right-to-left page turns

READ THIS BEFORE YOU CALL IT A MUSHAF - IT IS NOT ONE.

A real Madani Mushaf page is a fixed typographic object: specific words on
specific lines of specific pages, in the Uthmani glyph set, identical in every
printed copy. Reproducing that needs two things you do not have:

  1. a page-and-line layout table mapping every word to its position
  2. the QCF/Uthmani font that table is measured against

Your bundled quran-json has verse text only, and Amiri is not the Mushaf font.

So this view flows the text and paginates it to the READER'S SCREEN. It fits a
whole number of lines per page - up to 15, fewer on a short phone so nothing
clips - and never splits a line. But the breaks will not match a printed
Mushaf.

That matters. People who memorise do it by page position, and a view that looks
like a Mushaf while breaking lines somewhere else is worse than one that
plainly does not pretend. The footer shows "Page 3 of 12 - 15 lines" rather
than claiming a Mushaf page number.

If you want the real thing, it needs a licensed Madani layout dataset plus the
matching fonts. That is a data and licensing problem, not a UI one, and I can
wire it up in a day once you have the files.

PERFORMANCE NOTE: pagination lays the full surah out ONCE and reads its line
metrics. The obvious approach - re-measuring the remaining text for each page -
is quadratic and visibly janky on Al-Baqarah's 286 verses.
