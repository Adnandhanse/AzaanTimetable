=====================================================================
BOTH NISABS, USER SELECTS
=====================================================================
  Silver Nisab (595g)  |  Gold Nisab (85g)

Radio buttons, no default assumption beyond starting on silver. The two are NOT
equivalent, and the difference is not small. Worked example, verified:

  Rates: gold 7000/g (24K), silver 90/g (999)
  Holdings: 50g of 22K gold, 500g of 999 silver, 100000 cash, 50000 debts
  Net assets: 415,788

  SILVER NISAB  threshold  53,550  ->  APPLICABLE, zakat 10,394.71
  GOLD NISAB    threshold 595,000  ->  NOT APPLICABLE, zakat 0

Same assets, opposite answer. Which is why this is a radio button and not a
constant.

Your figures (595g / 85g) are used. Other published figures exist - 612.36g and
87.48g - and if your aalim wants those, they are two numbers in NisabBasisX.

=====================================================================
TWO CORRECTIONS TO YOUR SPEC
=====================================================================
1. SILVER HAS NO KARAT. Karat is a gold measure. Silver is graded by purity, and
   a jeweller's receipt says 999, 925 or 900. So:
       Gold   -> 24K (999) / 22K (916) / 21K (875) / 18K (750)
       Silver -> 999 (fine) / 925 (sterling) / 900

2. PURITY IS APPLIED TO THE VALUE. 50g of 22K gold is 45.83g of gold, so it is
   worth 320,833 at a 7000 pure rate, not 350,000. Valuing jewellery at the 24K
   rate would overstate what is owed by 8%.

=====================================================================
LIVE RATES - WHY THEY ARE NOT WIRED YET
=====================================================================
You asked for a daily live call. I have not shipped one, and I want to be
straight about why: my environment cannot reach any metals API, so any endpoint
I wired in would be UNTESTED. Untested network code fails at the worst possible
moment - while someone is calculating a financial obligation - and it fails
silently.

So: the user enters today's gold and silver rate once, it is cached with the
date, and the card shows how old it is. Over a day old and it says so in
amber. Works offline, no API key, no subscription, no new package.

TO ADD LIVE RATES: implement MetalRateService.fetchLive() to return one gram of
PURE metal and nothing else changes - the screen already calls it first and
falls back to the stored rate. KEEP the manual path as the fallback; it is what
makes the calculator dependable when a provider is down or a key expires.
