v13 - ALARM FAILURE FIXES
=========================

Full superset of v1-v12. Unzip over your repo root, overwriting.

=====================================================================
I BROKE YOUR BACKUP ALARM PATH IN v12. THAT IS ON ME.
=====================================================================
v12 created a new notification channel for the azan and DELETED the old one,
'prayer_times_channel'. But foreground_alarm_service.dart - the background
path that fires when the app has been killed, the one that matters most - was
still posting to that deleted channel.

On Android 8+, posting to a deleted channel fails SILENTLY. No error, no
notification, nothing in the log. That is very likely why your later prayers
produced nothing at all.

Fixed: the foreground service now uses the same channel constant as
NotificationService, with the azan sound attached.

=====================================================================
THE DESIGN FAULT BEHIND "ONE NOTIFICATION, THEN SILENCE"
=====================================================================
scheduleFromCache() called cancelAll() and then scheduled each prayer in a
loop. A single throw anywhere in that loop left EVERY alarm cancelled and
nothing rescheduled. main() then swallowed the exception with catch (_) {}.

So one bad sound resource, or one refused schedule call, silently wiped the
whole schedule. That matches your symptoms exactly: change the timings, get
nothing.

THREE CHANGES SO THIS CANNOT HAPPEN AGAIN:

1. THREE-TIER FALLBACK per alarm, instead of all-or-nothing:
     1. alarmClock + bundled azan
     2. alarmClock + default alert sound      (bad/missing azan resource)
     3. exactAllowWhileIdle + default sound   (alarmClock refused by the OS)
   An alarm that rings with the wrong sound beats one that does not ring.

2. ONE PRAYER FAILING NO LONGER ABORTS THE REST. Each is scheduled in its own
   try block; failures are collected, not fatal.

3. NOTHING IS SWALLOWED. Every run records what happened.

=====================================================================
SETTINGS > ALARM HEALTH NOW TELLS YOU WHAT WENT WRONG
=====================================================================
New "Last scheduling run" panel:
    Result         ok / partial / no cached schedule
    Alarms armed   how many actually took
    Method used    which of the three tiers worked
    FAILED         which prayers were refused, in red

The test alarm now REPORTS instead of just firing. If the system refuses all
three tiers, it says so - "The system REFUSED to schedule an alarm" - which is
the answer you needed when nothing happened after 60 seconds. That message
means the phone is blocking scheduling outright, and no app code fixes it;
it is the permissions above or Autostart.

Also added: "Re-arm alarms now" button.

=====================================================================
WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU INSTALL THIS
=====================================================================
1. Settings > Alarm health
2. Look at "Last scheduling run" - Alarms armed should be 5 or 6
3. Press the 60-second test, lock the phone
4. If it does not fire, come back to that screen and tell me EXACTLY what
   Result, Method used and FAILED say. That will identify the cause in one
   step instead of another guessing round.
