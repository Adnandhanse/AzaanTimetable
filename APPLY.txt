=====================================================================
THE "ISLAM CONNECT IS ACTIVE" NOTIFICATION
=====================================================================
I CANNOT REMOVE IT. Android does not allow it.

A foreground service MUST show a permanent notification — that is the deal the
OS makes in exchange for letting an app keep running in the background. There is
no flag to hide it.

AND THE SERVICE IS WHAT DELIVERS YOUR ALARMS. We established that over about
fifteen versions: on your phone the OS scheduler arms alarms and never fires
them, while this polling service works. Removing the service to hide the
notification would take the app straight back to no alarms at all.

SO I MADE IT WORTH HAVING. It now reads:

    Asr — in 2h 6m
    5:40 PM

instead of "Islam Connect is active". Updated once a minute — the text only
changes by the minute, and rewriting it every 20 seconds is three times the work
for no visible difference.

It follows the same Friday rule as the alarms: Juma counts down only on Friday,
Dhuhr does not count down on one.

ABOUT THE TIME UNDERNEATH: that is Android's own timestamp on the notification,
showing when the service started. flutter_foreground_task may expose a showWhen
flag to hide it — but I could not verify that parameter exists in the version you
are on, and three of your builds have already failed on unverified plugin APIs. I
am not guessing again. With the countdown in the text, the timestamp matters much
less anyway.

If you would rather it just said "Islam Connect" with no countdown, that is a
one-line change — say so.

FIVE LOGO OPTIONS
=====================================================================
Open logo-options/ALL_OPTIONS.png. Top row is large, bottom row is the SAME
logos at 96px — launcher size. That second row is the real test: a logo that
only works at 512px is not a logo.

  1  MIHRAB + CRESCENT   The arch already used throughout the app, with a
                         crescent inside. Most tied to the existing design.
  2  GIRIH STAR          Eight-point star, crescent in the centre. The most
                         traditionally Islamic, and the most distinctive at
                         small size.
  3  KUFIC MONOGRAM      A minaret as the "I", a crescent-like "C". The most
                         modern and the only one that reads as initials.
  4  COMPASS ROSE        Qibla and prayer times in one mark. The most literal
                         about what the app does.
  5  CRESCENT + BOOK     Prayer and reading — the two halves of the app.

All five use your exact palette: #0F5E3A, #C79A2E, #F0D48A.

They are SVG, so they scale to every launcher density without loss. Tell me which
one and I will produce the full Android icon set — every mipmap density, the
adaptive-icon foreground and background layers, and the Play Store 512px listing
icon.

MY PICK: 2, the girih star. It survives the shrink to 96px best, it does not look
like any other prayer app, and the eight-point star is unambiguous without being
a picture of a building.
