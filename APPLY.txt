TWO CAUSES, BOTH MINE.

--- CAUSE 1: alarmClock scheduling fails SILENTLY on your phone -------------
In v11 I switched to AndroidScheduleMode.alarmClock, calling it "the biggest
single win". On paper it is. In practice several Android ROMs accept
setAlarmClock and then never deliver it. It does not throw, so my three-tier
try/catch ladder could not detect it — scheduling reported success, tier 1,
and nothing ever fired. Your screenshot said "azan + alarmClock" and that was
the app confidently reporting a method that does nothing on your device.

FIXED: alarmClock is no longer the default. exactAllowWhileIdle is - weaker on
paper, actually delivers. There is a switch in Alarm health if you want to try
alarmClock on a different phone.

--- CAUSE 2: the thing that DID work was running on stale data --------------
The 8:20 alarm was not the scheduler. It was the foreground service, which
polls every 20 seconds and posts the notification itself. That is the one path
proven to work on your phone.

But its copy of your prayer times was only ever refreshed from the Home screen.
Save a new time in the admin panel and the service kept watching the OLD one.
8:20 was in its data; 8:24 never got there.

FIXED: ForegroundAlarmManager.refreshFromCache(), called on every scheduling
run, so the polling service and the scheduler can never disagree again.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD
=====================================================================

REDUNDANT ONE-SHOT ALARMS (ids 300+)
Each prayer is now also scheduled as explicit one-shots for the next 3 days,
separately from the repeating alarm. The repeating alarm depends on the plugin
re-arming itself after each firing; if that link breaks, everything after today
was being lost silently. Every app open pushes the 3-day window forward.

NEXT FIRE TIMES ARE NOW SHOWN
Alarm health lists "Maghrib -> tomorrow 20:24" for each prayer. Setting a time
that has already passed today is not a bug, but it looks exactly like one.
This may well explain the 8:24 case on its own.

SHARED CHANNEL CONSTANTS
NotificationService and the background isolate had their own copies of the
channel id and drifted apart in v12 — the foreground service posted to a
channel the other had deleted, and Android drops those silently. One constants
file now, so that class of bug cannot recur. It also breaks an import cycle I
had just created.

AZAN FALLBACK (from v15)
"Masjid has not uploaded a custom azan yet" was literal: with no upload the
ringing screen played NOTHING, and with an upload it streamed from Firebase
Storage at the exact moment the alarm fired. The bundled azan is now the floor.
