1. PRAYER ROWS - THE TIMES WERE TRUNCATING
=====================================================================
Your screenshot showed "12:30 …" and "11:30 …". Four columns — name, Arabic,
azan, jamat — do not fit on a 360dp screen.

THE ARABIC NAME IS NOW DROPPED WHEN JAMAT IS SHOWN. A cut-off prayer time is
worse than a missing decorative name, and the Arabic stays for every masjid that
has no jamat times, so nothing is lost there.

Also: "PM" is lowercased and the columns rebalanced to 3 : 4 : 4. Checked at
360dp — "12:40 pm" needs about 74dp and now has 91dp. Nothing truncates.

=====================================================================
The card is gone. There is now a megaphone icon with a count badge beneath the

The card sat between the banner and the prayer times — the busiest place on the

The icon only appears when an announcement exists.

3. URDU FONT IN THE QUR'AN - THE REAL CAUSE
=====================================================================
SurahDetailScreen was never told which translation it had been given. It just
rendered whatever text it received in the Latin style, so Urdu came out in the
wrong face — exactly what your second screenshot shows.

Rather than thread a language parameter through five screens, the style is now
chosen FROM THE TEXT: AppText.translationFor() checks whether the string is in
Arabic script and returns Nastaliq or Latin accordingly. That cannot fall out of
step with what is actually on screen, which is precisely how this broke.

Applied in the surah reader and the juz reader. Arabic ayat already use the
Uthmanic face.

=====================================================================
4. ADMIN STAYS LOGGED IN
=====================================================================
The number is saved on the device and checked against Firestore on launch. If
the masjid still lists that number, the dashboard opens directly.

If it no longer does — an admin removed, a masjid deleted — the saved number is
discarded rather than failing on every launch.

A LOGOUT BUTTON WAS REQUIRED AND IS NOW ON THE DASHBOARD. Without it, a shared
phone or a change of caretaker would leave the previous admin signed in forever.
That was not in your list, but persistent login without a way out is a trap.

7. HIJRI CALENDAR
=====================================================================
A month view with the Gregorian day under each Hijri date, Fridays outlined,
today filled. Month arrows step through.

Reached by TAPPING THE DATE ON THE BANNER — which previously opened an alert
telling you today's Hijri date, directly below the banner already showing it.
Also in Settings.

Built by walking real Gregorian days and asking each its Hijri date, rather than
iterating Hijri days: months are 29 or 30 depending on the year, and this cannot
produce a day that does not exist.

It says plainly that the dates are calculated and local moon sighting may differ
by a day. That is the single most common complaint about any Hijri calendar in
an app.

=====================================================================
8. LAST UPDATED PER MASJID
=====================================================================
Every prayer-time save stamps timesUpdatedAt from the SERVER clock, not the
device — a phone with the wrong date would otherwise record a masjid as updated
in 2019.

The admin dashboard shows "Updated 3 days ago" / "Updated 2 months ago" /
"Times never updated", in grey under a month, amber past a month, red past three.
Prayer times drift with the seasons, so a masjid untouched since spring is a real
problem, not a cosmetic one.

That is also the field you would query to find stale masjids to chase.
